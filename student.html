Laundry Instructions & Safety Policy

1. Machine Instructions: Inspect pockets for coins and sharp objects. Adhere to posted washer load limits. Do not overload drum.

2. Room Access & 10-Min Window: Your single-use QR unlocks the door 10 minutes before slot start. Access expires 10 minutes into the slot. Arrive promptly.

3. Safety & Privacy: Do not share your access QR code with others. IoT door events and camera audit logs are maintained for student dorm security.

4. Cancellation: If you cannot attend, cancel your booking so other students may use the room.
