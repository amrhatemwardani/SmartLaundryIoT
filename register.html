Privacy notice · project draft

This notice explains what information the dorm laundry service plans to use. Zewail City should review and approve the final notice before production.

Information used

Name, university ID and email, major, dorm class, phone and optional WhatsApp, account approval status, laundry tickets, payment references, support requests, and access/audit events.

Purpose

Verify student eligibility, approve accounts, manage bookings and payment, issue time-limited access QR codes, send service notifications, answer support requests, and protect the dorm laundry room.

Payment and QR safety

Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider. The QR uses a temporary opaque token and does not contain your name or university ID. Door scans and ticket events are kept for access auditing.

Who can see information

You can see your own details and tickets. Supervisors see information needed for their dorm duties. Main administrators manage the system. Sensitive actions should be recorded in an audit trail.

Retention and your choices

The university must set retention periods and a privacy contact before launch. Keep your information accurate and use support to report incorrect details. Never include passwords or full card data in a support request.

Draft only: these are planned uses for the student project, not a final university privacy notice.

Terms & conditions · project draft

These draft terms describe expected use of dorm laundry and need university review before launch.

Eligibility and accounts

Use your own Zewail City email and correct student details. Student accounts require email verification and supervisor approval. Do not share passwords, tickets, or door QR codes.

Tickets, payments, cancellations

A ticket reserves one washer and one stated time slot at the price shown before checkout. It is confirmed only after the server verifies payment. Cancellation, refunds, wallet use, and no-show rules must be shown before payment and follow the approved university policy.

Machine and room safety

Follow posted machine instructions, report faults or hazards, and never tamper with locks, scanners, devices, or sensors. Emergency exit hardware must work without the website, power to the controller, or network.

QR access

Use only your own valid QR during the ticket access window. The system records allowed and denied scans. Payment does not grant access outside the ticket window.

Service interruption

Machine sensor status can be delayed or unavailable. Report payment or access problems through support. The approved policy must define assistance and refund handling for outages.

Accepting these draft terms does not waive rights under applicable law or university policy. The final terms must be reviewed and approved by Zewail City.
